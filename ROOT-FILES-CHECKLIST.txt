After unzip, these should exist in THIS folder (Planning-Tool-x.x.x-windows-portable):

  Install-Offline.bat
  Run-Demo.bat
  Run-With-Outlook.bat
  Save-Conda-Path.bat   (or Write-CondaBase.bat / windows\Save-Conda-Path.bat)
  planning_tool\        (folder)
  offline\wheels\       (many .whl files, total zip ~8-9 MB)

If Save-Conda-Path.bat is missing but Install-Offline.bat succeeded,
check hidden file .conda-base — you may not need Save-Conda-Path at all.
